## How it works {#how-it-works}

### 1. Find {#step-find}
Search by name, task, license, format or size. Models are grouped by family. Order is never paid.

### 2. Check {#step-check}
Every model has a license and a signed manifest. Every file has a size and a sha256. You can check them with stock tools.

### 3. Download {#step-download}
Use our `fw.py` tool, the Hugging Face tools with one setting, or plain links. No account, no token.

### 4. Know the source {#step-source}
A badge says where the bytes come from: Hosted by Allternit, Community seeded, or From source. We check it when you ask, and a model with no live source moves to Wanted.

## Explore {#explore}

intro: The catalog is grouped into sections. Each section is a family of models, newest first. Modality is a filter, not a section.

- **Catalog**: all sections with counts, and filters for task, license, format, size and source.
- **Wanted**: models that lost their source or failed a check, with the reason.
- **Mirrors**: hosts that carry the bytes, and how to seed.
- **Ledger**: what Freeweights costs and who pays.
- **Docs**: download, verify, mirror, manifest spec, API.

live_numbers_note: The three live numbers on the home page (models listed, files verified, download starts) are read from the catalog and the counts API. Show nothing until they exist, never a made-up figure.

## Why you can trust it {#trust}

- We publish the license and a checksum with every file.
- The manifests are signed. You verify with a public key.
- The code is open and the costs are public. The ledger is a signed hash chain.
- We never host sexual-content models, child sexual abuse material, real-person face or voice models without documented consent, or models marketed for fraud, malware or attacks.
- Support goes first to running costs. It is not a charitable gift and is not tax-deductible.

Read the full Operating Statement for the promises and how we keep them.
