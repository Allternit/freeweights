# Freeweights Privacy Notice

Status: Draft. Owner: Allternit. Published at /privacy. Covers the hub, site and download Worker.

## Downloads need no account

You do not sign in, give an email or accept tracking to download a public model.

## What the Worker counts

- Download starts, per file and per day. This is a count, not a list of people.
- We do not store IP addresses with download records.
- We may hold an IP address briefly in rate-limit and abuse logs. These are deleted within 7 days unless an abuse case is open.
- No ads. No third-party trackers. No fingerprinting.

## Tokens

Some upstream hosts use tokens. A token that starts with `hf_` is rejected by the Worker. We do not store it, forward it or write it to any log. Do not send us your tokens.

## Accounts

There is no account before Phase 4. When accounts exist, this notice will be updated before they open and will list what we keep.

## Reports and mail

Takedown reports and mail to our addresses are kept as long as needed to handle them and for our legal records.

## Sharing

We do not sell data. We share data only with service providers that run the site (for example Cloudflare) or when the law requires it.
