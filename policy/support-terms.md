# Freeweights Support Terms

Status: Draft. Owner: Allternit. Shown wherever a support link appears.

Support goes to Allternit LLC. It is not a charitable gift and is not tax-deductible.

## Terms

1. **Support, not donation.** We call it "support". We do not call it a donation or a gift.
2. **Who gets it.** Allternit LLC, into a separate account used for Freeweights.
3. **How it is used.** First to the running costs in the public ledger (hosting, bandwidth, storage, domain, signing, scanning). Each dollar in and out is shown at /ledger.
4. **No perks.** Support buys no badge, ranking, placement, access or early features. Supporters are named only with consent.
5. **Refunds.** A supporter may ask for a refund within 30 days. We refund the full amount to the original payment method. Later refunds are at our discretion.
6. **No promise of outcome.** Support does not promise any feature, date or result.
7. **Records.** We keep payment records as the law requires. Payment data stays with the payment processor.
8. **Changes.** If a nonprofit takes over the commons, we will say how support moves, and supporters may ask for a refund first.

## Before the support link opens

- Separate LLC bank account in place.
- This page live.
- CPA's written view on how support money is treated.
- Minnesota ch. 309 check complete (below).

## DECISION (Allternit)

- DECISION (Allternit): Minnesota ch. 309 (charitable solicitation) check. Options: counsel confirms we are outside it, or we register or use an exemption. Draft default: no support link until counsel answers in writing.
- DECISION (Allternit): Refund window (30 days). Draft default: 30 days.
- DECISION (Allternit): One-time only, or one-time and monthly. Draft default: one-time first.
- DECISION (Allternit): Whether to name supporters publicly by consent. Draft default: yes, opt-in only.

## Verify with counsel

- Whether the wording avoids being a solicitation for charity under Minnesota ch. 309 and other states.
- Tax treatment of support money for the LLC and the S-corp (CPA).
- Payment processor rules for this use.
- Refund and sales-tax questions, if any.
- What happens to support money if a nonprofit takes the project.
